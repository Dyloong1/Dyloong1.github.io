Teaching Philosophy Statement

  1. I believe students learn best when: they are taught with latest knowledge from high level to details, and give them a playground or multiple approaches other than only words for them to understand and practice their knowledge. For example, when you teach some difficult knowledge, you give some visualization, you give some demos to start with.
  2. As an educator, my role is: teach my students to find their own first principles of all the domain, always learn high-level guidance of everything. I want my students to learn knowledge and more than just knowledge, such as how to acquire knowledge, how to question knowledge with a clear mind. Also maintain a great mental health of students.
  3. One way I connect course content to students’ prior knowledge or experience is: do extensions from their understanding, find interior similarities and differences. Always goes from high level, with clear motivation and clear chain of thought.
  4. I know that meaningful learning has happened when students always stay with the high level ideas, motivations and be able to response, question and have their own understanding.
  5. I will continue to develop my teaching by: first improving how I understand the world and knowledge, second keep following the SOTA methodology and studies, finally keep doing this by teaching more and more students and because of the heterogeneity of students, find out how to generalize my teaching ability to benefit as many students as I can.

Developing Pedagogical Content Knowledge (PCK)

How I learn to teach

I learn how to teach by observing how my previous instructor teach students, also those excellent presenters during conference, and imagine how I want my instructor to teach me if I am a student.

How it helps me develop PCK

learn experience, how they interact with people with different background, how they present the difficult knowledge and make it easier to learn.

How I can continue to develop my PCK

always keep learning SOTA methods, technology, research, keep communicate with other great teacher and attend conference.
